Who I Am

About Me

I’m a licensed Electronics Engineer (RECEng.) and NOC/OSP Manager at Nexlogic Telecommunications Network Inc., a Philippines-based Last Mile ISP with GPON/FTTH infrastructure across Olongapo City, Pampanga, Cavite, and Bulacan.

With 15+ years in ISP and telecom operations, I manage everything from BGP border routing (AS135025) co-located at Equinix SG1/SG3, to FTTH provisioning, Linux infrastructure, FreeRADIUS AAA, and internal tooling. I also consult for cable operators and SMEs through my own practice, Meneses IT System & Network Solutions.

I’m an IECEP member based in Olongapo City, Zambales. My current focus is building better operational tools, improving NOC observability, and automating ISP workflows with Python and Django.
